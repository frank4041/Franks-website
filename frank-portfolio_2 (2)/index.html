Personal Project • Hard Surface

Xoot

01

Brief

Xoot was just some experiment — a self-directed exploration into an ergonomic solution for on-screen tablets. It's an articulating monitor arm designed to hold a screen at whatever angle actually feels comfortable, instead of forcing you into one fixed position. No brief, no client — just an excuse to push hard-surface modelling and mechanical detailing further.

02

Renders

Close-up studies of the hinge, joint, and tilt mechanism.

Motion Design

Carril Intro Video

01

Brief

Carril Agency came to us wanting a short film that felt like the start of every road trip: one small car, one big open sky, and that first stretch of highway where anything feels possible. The brief was simple: make it warm, make it a little funny, and make people want to get in the car.

02

Concept

Before touching final renders, we locked the world down on paper: storyboarding the beats, sketching the car's personality through its stickers and plates, and building out the "Carril" brand world that would ride along with it.

03

Work Bench Renders

Early clay-shaded turntables let us check the car's proportions, camera moves, and timing before any lighting or colour was locked in.

04

Styleframes

With the world and the car locked, we pushed single frames to final quality to define the lighting, colour, and mood before animating the full sequence.

05

Full Video

The finished film, brief to final grade.

Personal Project

What if: Nike headset

01

Concept

A personal what if: what would a pair of Nike over-ear headphones look and feel like. The idea started as a quick sketch exploring how Nike's running DNA, volt accents, mesh textures, expanding headband could translate from footwear into audio, then pushed through a couple of colourways before picking a direction to take into 3D.

02

Styleframes

Final beauty renders used to lock the lighting and colour grade before animating the full video.

03

Full Video

The finished spot, concept to final render.

In-house • Fintech

Flux

01

Brief

Flux is a fintech app for sending, spending, banking, and investing from one place: instant transfers across 40+ countries, virtual dollar cards for spending online, free local bank transfers, and dollar or crypto savings built in. I worked in Flux for about a year as part of the in-house design team, across motion design and icons, helping bring the product's day-to-day feel to life.

02

Motion Design

Short-form motion pieces made for the app and its social channels.

03

Icons

Icon sets designed for the app's product surfaces.

ABOUT ME

I'm a multidisciplinary artist obsessed with the intersection of technology and emotion. Whether it's a hyper-realistic product render or a stylized character, I focus on making pixels feel tangible.
